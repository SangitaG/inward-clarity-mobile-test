THE UNSEEN PROCESS

We all experience life
in a unique way.

LIFE HAPPENS
WE PERCEIVE
WE RESPOND
WE EXPERIENCE LIFE

Every experience we have moves through an unseen process. Most of the time, we are not aware it is happening.

We notice life
through our senses.

We interpret what we notice
through the beliefs, experiences,
habits, and conditioning we have
developed over the course of
our lives.

The meaning we create
shapes how we respond.

Our response is expressed through our

THOUGHTS
EMOTIONS
DECISIONS
BEHAVIORS
ACTIONS

Together, how we perceive and respond
shapes our experience of life.

Most of us have never been taught to observe this process. Instead, we simply experience its results.
