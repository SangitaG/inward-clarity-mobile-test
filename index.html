WHY I FOUNDED

Inward Clarity.

I learned through my own life that finding our way forward begins with learning to see ourselves more clearly.

◆

My life has taken me through different cultures, engineering, motherhood, shifting roles, and unexpected transitions. Through each chapter, I found myself turning inward, seeking to understand what I was experiencing and how I needed to grow to meet life well.

Over time, meditation, self-development, coaching, and NLP deepened that understanding and gave language and structure to something I had been discovering throughout my life.

When my children left home, I entered another transition. I knew I wanted the next chapter of my life to be about helping people, although I didn’t yet know what form that would take.

My coaching and NLP training, my own journey of growth, and what I was seeing happening in the world came together to create Inward Clarity.
